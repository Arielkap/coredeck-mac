Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 • Controller: Armako Limited (armako.co.uk)

1. Zero-Telemetry Philosophy

CoreDeck is built as a local-first native macOS application (Swift 6 + SwiftUI). We do not collect analytics, behavioral telemetry, crash dumps containing personal data, process lists, disk contents, or advertising identifiers.

2. Local System & AI VRAM Operations

Memory telemetry (host_statistics64), swap usage, APFS disk space, process lists, Darwin IOKit Coffee Mode assertions, and Eject AI VRAM actions (including requests to your local Ollama daemon at http://localhost:11434) run strictly on your own Mac. None of this information ever leaves your device.

3. Fleet Telemetry (SSH & Self-Hosted Agents)

If you configure remote Linux VPS monitoring, host addresses, SSH credentials/keys, and agent tokens remain stored locally on your Mac. Connections are initiated directly from your Mac to the target servers you configure. Armako Limited never receives, proxies, or has access to your server credentials or /proc metrics.

4. Payments, Licensing & Website

One-time Pro Lifetime purchases ($14.99 USD) are processed securely by Stripe (Stripe Privacy Policy). Armako Limited does not store or process raw payment card details.

Upon checkout completion, Stripe provides your email and transaction confirmation strictly to issue and restore your cryptographic Ed25519 license key (CORE-PRO-XXXX-XXXX). License keys are verified offline inside the CoreDeck macOS app without background tracking.

5. Your Rights & Contact (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR and applicable data protection laws, you may request access, correction, or deletion of purchase/support correspondence at any time.
